I've been meaning to start a blog for a while. Most of what I read online is written at the speed of feeds: reaction snippets that barely outlive the hour they were posted. I want a place to write at the speed of thought.

So: this is where I'll put longer pieces about software, and whatever else stays on my mind past the first draft. No schedule, no theme of the month. Just writing in public, one post at a time.

If you found this via RSS, welcome. There's an Archive link up top for everything that comes after.
